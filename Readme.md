# New Store Launch Feasibility & Expansion Strategy

## Project Overview

This project evaluates historical sales and profitability performance
across Indian markets to identify high-potential locations for a
potential new-store launch.

The analysis uses 100,000 sales records across 10 states and compares
sales, profit, orders and profit margin across states, regions,
categories and years.

## Business Problem

The business needs to identify which existing market shows the
strongest historical performance and should therefore be considered
for further investigation before launching a new store.

## Objectives

- Compare state-wise sales and profitability
- Evaluate regional and category performance
- Analyse historical yearly trends
- Identify high-potential markets
- Provide an evidence-based expansion recommendation

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and lookup functions
- Data aggregation
- Charts and dashboarding
- Business Analysis

## Key Result

Rajasthan emerged as the leading historical market candidate based on
the project's screening criteria.

Key historical figures:
- Sales: INR 250.84 Cr
- Profit: INR 37.55 Cr
- Overall Profit Margin: 15%
- Records analysed: 100,000
- States evaluated: 10
